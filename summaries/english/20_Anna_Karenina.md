# Anna Karenina
**Author:** Leo Tolstoy

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A tragedy of passion, societal hypocrisy, and marriage, contrasting Anna's destructive extramarital love with Levin's rural search for faith, family, and spiritual peace.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Anna abandons her loveless marriage to Karenin for Count Vronsky, facing total social ostracization.
- Parallel plot of Konstantin Levin finding peace through rural labor, nature, and marriage to Kitty.
- Anna's descent into jealousy, isolation, and her climactic suicide under the train.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
All happy families are alike; each unhappy family is unhappy in its own way.
