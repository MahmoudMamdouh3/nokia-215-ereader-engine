# Animal Farm
**Author:** George Orwell

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A satirical allegorical novella exposing how revolutionary idealism is inevitably corrupted by ruthless dictators into tyrannical totalitarianism.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Animals overthrow their drunken human master Mr. Jones to establish equality.
- The pigs (Napoleon and Snowball) seize leadership; Napoleon exiles Snowball with guard dogs.
- The Seven Commandments are gradually altered until: 'All animals are equal, but some animals are more equal than others.'
- The pigs walk on two legs and become indistinguishable from their human oppressors.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Revolutions only result in a change of masters if citizens fail to remain eternally vigilant against power.
