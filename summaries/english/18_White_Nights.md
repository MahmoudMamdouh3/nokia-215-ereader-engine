# White Nights
**Author:** Fyodor Dostoevsky

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A sentimental, lyrical novella exploring romantic idealism, loneliness, and the bittersweet transience of pure connection in St. Petersburg during the summer solstice.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- The unnamed Dreamer wanders isolated through the streets of St. Petersburg.
- He meets Nastenka weeping on a bridge; they share their inner worlds over four nights.
- Nastenka loves another man who returns; she departs, leaving the Dreamer with grateful memories.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
A whole moment of bliss: is that too little, even for the whole of a man's life?
