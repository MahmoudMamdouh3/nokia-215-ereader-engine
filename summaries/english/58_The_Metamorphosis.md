# The Metamorphosis
**Author:** Franz Kafka

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
Gregor Samsa wakes to find himself transformed into a monstrous insect. An allegorical masterpiece exploring alienation, exploitation, and the conditional nature of familial affection.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Gregor, sole breadwinner for his family, awakens as a giant vermin; his immediate worry is missing work.
- His family's gradual shift from shock to disgust, neglect, and outright hostility.
- Gregor starves himself to death, releasing his family, who immediately celebrate their new freedom.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
When a person can no longer produce or provide economic utility, society and even family often discard them.
