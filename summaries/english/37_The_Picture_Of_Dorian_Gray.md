# The Picture of Dorian Gray
**Author:** Oscar Wilde

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A decadent philosophical fable on aestheticism, vanity, and the moral corruption of a man whose painted portrait ages and rots while his physical body remains eternally young.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Lord Henry Wotton seduces Dorian into pure hedonistic aestheticism.
- Basil Hallward paints Dorian's peerless portrait; Dorian wishes the painting age in his place.
- Dorian engages in betrayal, murder, and vice without a single physical blemish.
- In agony, Dorian slashes the painting, inadvertently stabbing his own withered, grotesque heart.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
What does it profit a man if he gains the whole world and loses his own soul?
