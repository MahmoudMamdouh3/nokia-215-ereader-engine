# Love in the Time of Cholera
**Author:** Gabriel García Márquez

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A lush, poetic meditation on romantic devotion, patience, and aging. Florentino Ariza waits fifty-one years, nine months, and four days to reunite with his true love, Fermina Daza.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Young passionate love thwarted by social class; Fermina marries the respectable Dr. Juvenal Urbino.
- Florentino pursues hundreds of affairs while keeping his heart exclusively devoted to Fermina.
- Following Urbino's death, elderly Florentino re-courts Fermina.
- They sail indefinitely up the river under the yellow flag of cholera, choosing eternal love.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Age and physical decay cannot diminish the enduring vitality of passionate, committed love.
