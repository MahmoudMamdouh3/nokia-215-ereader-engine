# The Great Gatsby
**Author:** F. Scott Fitzgerald

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A lyrical critique of the corruption of the American Dream in the 1920s Jazz Age, exposing the hollowness of material excess and the impossibility of resurrecting the past.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Jay Gatsby's lavish mansion and yearning for the green light on Daisy Buchanan's dock.
- The careless, destructive entitlement of Tom and Daisy Buchanan.
- Myrtle Wilson's tragic death and Gatsby's solitary murder in his pool.
- The tragic emptiness of his funeral: all who partied abandon him.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
So we beat on, boats against the current, borne back ceaselessly into the past.
