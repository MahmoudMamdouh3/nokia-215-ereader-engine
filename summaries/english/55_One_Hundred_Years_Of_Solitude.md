# One Hundred Years of Solitude
**Author:** Gabriel García Márquez

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
The crowning achievement of magical realism, chronicling the rise and apocalyptic fall of the Buendía family in Macondo across seven generations cursed by incestuous solitude.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Founding of Macondo by José Arcadio Buendía and Úrsula Iguarán.
- Colonel Aureliano Buendía fights 32 civil wars and loses them all.
- The Banana Company massacre: state terrorism wiped from memory.
- Deciphering Melquíades' prophecy as a hurricane sweeps Macondo from the earth.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Races condemned to one hundred years of solitude did not have a second opportunity on earth.
