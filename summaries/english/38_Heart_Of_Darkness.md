# Heart of Darkness
**Author:** Joseph Conrad

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A haunting descent up the Congo River uncovering the savage hypocrisy of European colonial imperialism and the terrifying psychological abyss lurking within the human soul.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Marlow journeys to Africa as a steamboat captain for an ivory trading company.
- He witnesses the brutal exploitation and horrific treatment of native Africans.
- He encounters Mr. Kurtz, a charismatic ivory agent worshipped as a demigod by local tribes.
- Kurtz's dying, whispered revelation of truth: 'The horror! The horror!'

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Civilization is a fragile veneer; remove societal constraints, and the primitive darkness within humanity erupts.
