# The Lord of the Rings
**Author:** J.R.R. Tolkien

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
The definitive epic fantasy myth of Middle-earth. The corrupting burden of absolute power, the selfless heroism of the humble, and the enduring light of friendship against overwhelming darkness.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Frodo Baggins undertakes the perilous quest to cast the One Ring into Mount Doom.
- The Fellowship of the Ring fractures under war, temptation, and sacrifice.
- Aragorn claims his ancestral kingship; Rohan and Gondor unite against Mordor.
- Pity and mercy: Gollum's desperate treachery inadvertently completes the Ring's destruction.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Even the smallest person can alter the course of the future.
