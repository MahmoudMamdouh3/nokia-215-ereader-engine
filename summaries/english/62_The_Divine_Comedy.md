# The Divine Comedy (Inferno, Purgatorio, Paradiso)
**Author:** Dante Alighieri

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
An allegorical poetic tour through Hell, Purgatory, and Heaven guided by Virgil and Beatrice, charting the soul's ascent from sin through moral purification to divine contemplation.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Inferno: Dante traverses the nine circles of Hell, observing the precise poetic justice (contrapasso) of sins.
- Purgatorio: Climbing the mountain of purification where repentant souls cleanse their vices.
- Paradiso: Guided by Beatrice through the celestial spheres, culminating in the vision of Divine Light.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Love moves the sun and the other stars. Moral redemption requires confronting the horror of sin and striving toward truth.
