# Lolita
**Author:** Vladimir Nabokov

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A controversial, stylistically dazzling novel examining aesthetic obsession, manipulation, and the tragic destruction of a child's innocence by an unreliable, solipsistic narrator.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Humbert Humbert's predatory obsession with young girls ('nymphets') and 12-year-old Dolores Haze.
- His manipulation and cross-country road trip abusing Dolores.
- Dolores's escape and Humbert's final recognition that he destroyed her genuine childhood.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Dazzling prose and aesthetic rationalizations cannot disguise moral monstrosity and the irreparable harm done to innocence.
