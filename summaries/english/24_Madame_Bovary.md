# Madame Bovary
**Author:** Gustave Flaubert

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A ruthless portrait of romantic delusion and bourgeois banality. Emma Bovary's quest for romantic passion leads to adultery, financial ruin, and catastrophic despair.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Emma's marriage to the dull, kind doctor Charles Bovary fails to satisfy her romantic fantasies.
- Affairs with Rodolphe and Léon reveal the same emptiness she sought to escape.
- Predatory moneylender Lheureux exploits her debt, leading to her agonizing suicide by arsenic.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Romantic escapism that despises the reality of daily duty inevitably destroys both self and family.
