# Moby-Dick; or, The Whale
**Author:** Herman Melville

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
An encyclopedic metaphysical tragedy depicting Captain Ahab's monomaniacal vengeance against the giant white whale, exploring human obsession and cosmic indifference.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Ishmael boards the doomed whaling ship Pequod with pagan harpooner Queequeg.
- Ahab hijacks the commercial voyage into a fanatical personal vendetta.
- The white whale shatters the ship, drowning all crew; Ishmael survives on Queequeg's coffin.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Blind obsession destroys the seeker and all who follow him.
