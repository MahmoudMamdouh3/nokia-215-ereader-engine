# The Master and Margarita
**Author:** Mikhail Bulgakov

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A brilliant satirical fantasy attacking Soviet censorship and materialism, juxtaposing the Devil's havoc in 1930s Moscow with Pontius Pilate's agonizing trial of Yeshua in ancient Jerusalem.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Woland (Satan) arrives in Moscow and exposes the greed, hypocrisy, and cowardice of the Soviet elite.
- The Master, a persecuted novelist, is committed to an asylum for writing about Pontius Pilate.
- Margarita makes a pact with Woland to become a witch, save the Master, and achieve eternal peace.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Manuscripts don't burn. Truth and genuine art endure beyond political tyranny.
