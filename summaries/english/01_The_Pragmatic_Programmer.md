# The Pragmatic Programmer (20th Anniversary Edition)
**Author:** David Thomas & Andrew Hunt

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
Pragmatic programming is about continuous professional learning, taking responsibility for your code, designing modular software, and focusing on user value rather than dogmatic methodologies.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Care about your craft and think constantly about your decisions.
- Fix broken windows immediately to prevent technical debt.
- Apply DRY (Don't Repeat Yourself) across all system specifications.
- Design orthogonal, decoupled modules to avoid ripple effects.
- Practice assertive programming: fail fast and loud when invariants fail.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Invest constantly in your knowledge portfolio. Software is not rigid engineering; it is gardening that requires daily pruning and cultivation.
