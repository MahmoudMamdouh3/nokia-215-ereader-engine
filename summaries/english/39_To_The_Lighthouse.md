# To the Lighthouse
**Author:** Virginia Woolf

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A modernist stream-of-consciousness meditation on the passage of time, family dynamics, grief, gender roles, and the transient nature of human existence centered around the Ramsay family.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- The Window: domestic tensions and warm matriarchal presence of Mrs. Ramsay on the Isle of Skye.
- Time Passes: ten years vanish in poetic brevity; war, decay, and death sweep through the empty house.
- The Lighthouse: the remaining family finally sails to the lighthouse while artist Lily Briscoe completes her vision.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Love and genuine artistic vision provide the only fleeting permanence against the unstoppable erosion of time.
