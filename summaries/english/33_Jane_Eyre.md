# Jane Eyre
**Author:** Charlotte Brontë

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A groundbreaking feminist bildungsroman chronicling an orphaned governess's fierce moral integrity, spiritual independence, and enduring love for the brooding Mr. Rochester.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Jane survives cruel abuse at Gateshead and Lowood School with unyielding inner dignity.
- As governess at Thornfield Hall, she falls in love with Edward Rochester.
- On their wedding day, Rochester's secret mad wife in the attic (Bertha Mason) is revealed.
- Jane refuses to be his mistress, departs in poverty, but returns once they are moral equals.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Never sacrifice your moral conscience or self-respect for emotional convenience or romantic desire.
