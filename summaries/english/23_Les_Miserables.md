# Les Misérables
**Author:** Victor Hugo

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
An epic social manifesto advocating mercy, redemption, and human dignity against legal cruelty, poverty, and institutional callousness in 19th-century France.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Jean Valjean's transformation from hardened convict to saintly benefactor through Bishop Myriel's grace.
- Inspector Javert's relentless, unbending legalism.
- Fantine's sacrifice and Cosette's rescue.
- The 1832 Paris June Rebellion and Marius's idealism.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
To love another person is to see the face of God. Grace is infinitely higher than mere law.
