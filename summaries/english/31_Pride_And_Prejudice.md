# Pride and Prejudice
**Author:** Jane Austen

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A sparkling social comedy of manners and courtship examining how false first impressions, personal pride, and hasty prejudice obstruct genuine love and mutual respect.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Elizabeth Bennet's quick wit and prejudice against the wealthy, aloof Mr. Darcy.
- Darcy's aristocratic pride humbled by Elizabeth's rejection.
- Darcy secretly rescues the Bennet family from social ruin over Lydia's elopement.
- Elizabeth overcomes her misjudgment, recognizing Darcy's true nobility.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
True self-knowledge requires overcoming both the vanity of your own intellect and premature judgments of others.
