# Beloved
**Author:** Toni Morrison

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A Pulitzer Prize-winning masterpiece exploring the indelible psychic trauma, generational grief, and haunting legacy of American slavery.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Sethe, a former enslaved woman, lives haunted by the ghost of the baby daughter she killed to save from slavery.
- A mysterious young woman named Beloved arrives, embodying physical manifested trauma and guilt.
- The community of Black women unites to exorcise the ghost and allow Sethe to claim her own selfhood.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Freeing yourself was one thing; claiming ownership of that freed self was another.
