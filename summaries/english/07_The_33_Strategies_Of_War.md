# The 33 Strategies of War
**Author:** Robert Greene

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
Life is subtle psychological warfare. Military strategy applied to professional and social battlefields grants resilience, clarity, and victory.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Declare war on your weaknesses: avoid complacency.
- Create a sense of urgency (Death Ground Strategy).
- Know your enemy: psychological profiling.
- Trade space for time: counter-attack when opponents exhaust themselves.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Strategy is a mental framework of detachment and adaptability, not a rigid set of rules.
