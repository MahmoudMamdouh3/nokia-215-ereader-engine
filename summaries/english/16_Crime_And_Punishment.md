# Crime and Punishment
**Author:** Fyodor Dostoevsky

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
Rationalist utilitarianism collapses before the moral law of the human conscience. True redemption requires confessing guilt, accepting suffering, and spiritual resurrection.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Raskolnikov's 'Extraordinary Man' theory leads him to murder an old pawnbroker.
- Psychological torment and alienation replace his expected triumph.
- The intellectual duel with magistrate Porfiry Petrovich.
- Sonya Marmeladova's faith guides Raskolnikov to confession and Siberian exile.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
No intellect can outsmart the human soul's intrinsic need for moral harmony.
