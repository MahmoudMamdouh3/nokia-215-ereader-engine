# The Holy Quran: The Final Revelation
**Author:** Divine Revelation

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
The central religious text of Islam, revealed to the Prophet Muhammad over 23 years. A comprehensive spiritual, moral, legal, and linguistic guide establishing monotheism, justice, mercy, and human purpose.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- 114 Surahs categorized into Makki (theology, afterlife, ethics) and Madani (legislation, governance, community).
- Core doctrine of Tawhid (Absolute Oneness of God).
- Balance of divine justice, universal mercy, and individual moral accountability.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
A transformative blueprint for purifying the heart, living with justice, and fulfilling humanity's covenant with the Creator.
