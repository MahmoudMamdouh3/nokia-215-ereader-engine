# The Grapes of Wrath
**Author:** John Steinbeck

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A harrowing, compassionate epic of the Joad family driven from the Dust Bowl during the Great Depression, exploring the resilience of human solidarity against capitalist exploitation.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- The Joads journey along Route 66 to California seeking promised farm work.
- They face exploitation, starvation, police brutality, and squalid migrant camps.
- Tom Joad embraces preacher Jim Casy's vision of universal human brotherhood.
- Rose of Sharon's climactic, transcendent act of nursing a starving stranger.
