# The Old Man and the Sea
**Author:** Ernest Hemingway

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A heroic, spare novella of human endurance and dignity. An aging Cuban fisherman battles an immense marlin in the Gulf Stream, asserting that man can be destroyed, but not defeated.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Santiago has gone 84 days without a fish; he ventures far into the Gulf Stream alone.
- A monumental three-day physical and spiritual duel with a giant marlin.
- He kills the fish with reverence, only for sharks to devour the carcass on the voyage home.
- He returns with only the skeleton, yet retains undefeated spirit and the boy Manolin's devotion.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Man is not made for defeat. A man can be destroyed but not defeated.
