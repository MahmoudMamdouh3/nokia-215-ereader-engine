# Ulysses
**Author:** James Joyce

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
The pinnacle of modernist literature, chronicling a single day (June 16, 1904) in the life of Leopold Bloom in Dublin, paralleling Homer's Odyssey through revolutionary stream-of-consciousness.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Leopold Bloom (Odysseus) traverses Dublin as an ad canvasser, gentle, observant, and cuckolded.
- Stephen Dedalus (Telemachus) seeks intellectual independence and a spiritual father.
- Molly Bloom's (Penelope) unpunctuated final monologue affirming life and love: 'yes I said yes I will Yes.'

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
The heroic epic is found not in mythical battles, but in the mundane, tender, and chaotic thoughts of ordinary daily human life.
