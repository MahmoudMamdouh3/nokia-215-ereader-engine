# Don Quixote de la Mancha
**Author:** Miguel de Cervantes

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
The first modern novel: a hilarious and deeply poignant satire of chivalric romance, contrasting noble idealism with harsh realism through the knight-errant and his earthy squire.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Alonso Quixano reads chivalric books until madness drives him to roam as Don Quixote.
- Sancho Panza serves as his pragmatic, proverb-spouting squire.
- Tilting at windmills believed to be giants; seeing peasant girls as noble ladies.
- Quixote recovers his sanity on his deathbed, yet Sancho mourns the loss of noble dreaming.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Madness that strives to right wrongs and protect the weak is nobler than the sane cynicism that tolerates cruelty.
