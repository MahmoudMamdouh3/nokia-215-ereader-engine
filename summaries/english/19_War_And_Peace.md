# War and Peace
**Author:** Leo Tolstoy

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A panoramic epic of Russian society during the Napoleonic Wars, interwoven with deep philosophical reflections on history, free will, and the search for authentic meaning.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- The interconnected lives of the Rostovs, Bolkonskys, and Bezukhovs.
- Pierre Bezukhov's spiritual quest for purpose.
- Prince Andrei Bolkonsky's pursuit of military glory and ultimate transcendence.
- Natasha Rostova's vitality, mistakes, and emotional maturity.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
History is not driven by 'great men' like Napoleon, but by the collective, minute decisions of millions of ordinary individuals.
