# Mrs. Dalloway
**Author:** Virginia Woolf

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A single day in post-WWI London contrasting Clarissa Dalloway's high-society party preparations with shell-shocked veteran Septimus Warren Smith's psychological collapse.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Stream-of-consciousness narrative moving seamlessly between minds, memory, and street sounds.
- Clarissa reflects on choices made, lost loves (Peter Walsh), and social masks.
- Septimus suffers from PTSD, dismissed by arrogant doctors, and jumps to his death.
- Clarissa hears of the suicide at her party, understanding his sacrifice as an act of defiance against a stifling world.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Life is a luminous halo; every ordinary hour contains the eternity of human joy and grief.
