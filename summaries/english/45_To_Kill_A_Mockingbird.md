# To Kill a Mockingbird
**Author:** Harper Lee

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A moral masterpiece seen through Scout Finch's eyes, chronicling her lawyer father Atticus's heroic defense of a falsely accused Black man in the racially segregated American South.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Scout and Jem grow up learning empathy in Maycomb, Alabama.
- Atticus defends Tom Robinson despite public hatred and threats.
- Tom is unjustly convicted by an all-white jury, exposing entrenched systemic prejudice.
- Reclusive neighbor Boo Radley saves the children, proving the gentleness of misunderstood souls.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Real courage is when you know you're licked before you begin, but you begin anyway and see it through no matter what.
