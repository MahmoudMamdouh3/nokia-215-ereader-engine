# The Iliad
**Author:** Homer

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
The epic poem of the wrath of Achilles during the final weeks of the Trojan War, exploring honor, the brutality of combat, mortality, and the shared tragedy of humanity.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Achilles withdraws from battle following a humiliating dispute with Agamemnon.
- Hector leads the Trojans to the brink of burning the Greek fleet.
- The death of Patroclus re-ignites Achilles' monstrous rage; he slays Hector and desecrates his corpse.
- King Priam begs Achilles for Hector's body; they weep together over the shared grief of mortal life.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
War brings only grief to victors and vanquished alike; empathy across enmity is humanity's highest glory.
