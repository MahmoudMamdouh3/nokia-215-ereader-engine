# Nineteen Eighty-Four (1984)
**Author:** George Orwell

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
The ultimate warning against totalitarian tyranny. The Party controls past, present, language, and truth, breaking individual humanity through surveillance and fear.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Newspeak, Doublethink, and the Ministry of Truth rewriting facts.
- Winston Smith's secret diary and illicit love affair with Julia.
- Betrayal by O'Brien and torture in Room 101.
- Total surrender: Winston betrays Julia and learns to 'love Big Brother.'

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Freedom is the freedom to say that two plus two make four. If that is granted, all else follows.
