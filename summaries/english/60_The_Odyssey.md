# The Odyssey
**Author:** Homer

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
The foundational epic of Western literature: Odysseus's ten-year perilous voyage home to Ithaca after the Trojan War, demonstrating cunning, endurance, and the sanctity of home and family.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Odysseus faces mythical monsters (Polyphemus the Cyclops, Circe, the Sirens, Scylla and Charybdis).
- Penelope's steadfast fidelity weaving and unweaving her burial shroud to fend off suitors.
- Telemachus's journey into manhood.
- Odysseus returns in disguise, slaughters the suitors, and reclaims his kingdom and wife.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Endurance and intellect (metis) are far greater weapons than raw physical strength.
