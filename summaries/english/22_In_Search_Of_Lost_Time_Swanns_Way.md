# In Search of Lost Time: Swann's Way
**Author:** Marcel Proust

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A monumental exploration of involuntary memory, time, desire, and art. The taste of a madeleine dipped in tea unleashes the narrator's entire vanished childhood in Combray.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Involuntary memory vs. intellectual recall: sensory triggers resurrect lost time.
- Swann in Love: Charles Swann's obsessive, agonizing jealousy over the courtesan Odette de Crécy.
- The transformation of fleeting, decaying life into immortal art.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
The only real voyage of discovery consists not in seeking new landscapes, but in having new eyes.
