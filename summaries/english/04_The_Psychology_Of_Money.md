# The Psychology of Money
**Author:** Morgan Housel

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
Financial success is driven primarily by behavioral discipline, humility, and patience, rather than mathematical genius or financial acumen.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- The power of compounding: endurance across decades drives exponential returns.
- Getting wealthy requires risk-taking; staying wealthy requires frugality and humility.
- Freedom to control your time is the highest dividend wealth provides.
- Room for error and margin of safety protect against ruin.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Wealth is what you do not spend. True wealth is flexibility, autonomy, and security.
