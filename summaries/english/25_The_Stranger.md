# The Stranger (L'Étranger)
**Author:** Albert Camus

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
The foundational novel of the Absurd. Meursault refuses to lie about his feelings or conform to societal pretenses, choosing total honesty even when condemned to death.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Meursault impassively attends his mother's funeral and shoots an Arab on a blindingly hot Algiers beach.
- Society condemns him not for the killing, but because he didn't weep at his mother's grave.
- Confronting the prison chaplain, Meursault embraces the benign indifference of the universe.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
A hero who dies because he refuses to play the game of societal hypocrisy.
