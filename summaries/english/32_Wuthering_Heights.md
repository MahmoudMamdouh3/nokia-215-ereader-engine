# Wuthering Heights
**Author:** Emily Brontë

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
A ferocious, gothic masterpiece of all-consuming, destructive passion and multi-generational revenge between Heathcliff and Catherine Earnshaw on the Yorkshire moors.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Heathcliff's orphan adoption, abuse by Hindley, and elemental bond with Catherine.
- Catherine marries Edgar Linton for social status, shattering Heathcliff's soul.
- Heathcliff returns wealthy and enacts a methodical, demonic vengeance on both families.
- Catherine's daughter and Hindley's son eventually break the cycle of hate with gentle love.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Obsessive love that turns into bitter revenge destroys everything in its path, including the avenger.
