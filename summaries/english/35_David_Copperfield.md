# David Copperfield
**Author:** Charles Dickens

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
Dickens's most personal, semi-autobiographical masterpiece following David's journey through childhood trauma, debtor's prisons, perseverance, and emotional maturity.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- David endures the cruel cruelty of stepfather Mr. Murdstone and child labor in a London factory.
- The unforgettable friendship with eccentric optimist Mr. Micawber.
- Flight to Aunt Betsey Trotwood and his emergence as an author.
- Loss of youthful romantic illusion (Dora) and finding mature companionship with Agnes Wickfield.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Perseverance, moral earnestness, and a resilient heart can turn the darkest childhood neglect into literary and human triumph.
