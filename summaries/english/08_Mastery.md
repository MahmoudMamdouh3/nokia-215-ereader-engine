# Mastery
**Author:** Robert Greene

## Executive Summary & Core Premise
Mastery is not born of innate genius, but of a rigorous apprenticeship, intense focus, creative experimentation, and thousands of hours of deliberate practice.

## Key Structural Breakdown & Analysis
- Discover your Life's Task: align with your deepest interests.
- The Apprenticeship Phase: endure tedious foundational training without ego.
- Absorb the master's power: mentorship.
- The Creative-Active Phase: synthesize rules to break new ground.

## Actionable Takeaways & Lessons
Time and intense focus transform ordinary diligence into intuitive mastery.
